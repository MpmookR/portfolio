The following section presents the full results of the user survey conducted to evaluate the
SoPup prototype. While Chapter 2 provided a summary of the key findings, this appendix
contains the complete data, charts, and selected participant comments for transparency
and further reference.
Results are organised in the same structure as the questionnaire:
1. About You: demographic context and prior experience with dog socialisation apps.
2. Onboarding Experience: usability and clarity during profile setup.
3. Puppy Mode: accessibility of educational content and clarity of vaccination/status
display.
4. Social Mode: evaluation of filters, profiles, matchmaking, chat, and meet-up rules.
5. Overall Impressions: favourite features, improvement suggestions, and likelihood
of adoption.

Both quantitative (Likert scale, multiple-choice) and qualitative (open-ended feedback)
responses are included. Figures C.x show the charts automatically generated from the
survey form, supported by a narrative explanation for each section.

About you section
The survey responses indicated that all participants (100%) currently own a dog, ensuring
that feedback was provided by relevant stakeholders with first-hand experience in dog
ownership. All respondents reported their dogs as being over six months old, meaning the
insights were primarily from owners of adult dogs rather than puppies. When asked about
prior experience with dog socialisation or meetup applications, 42.9% had previously used
such apps, while 57.1% had not. This mix of experienced and first-time users provided a
balanced perspective, enabling the identification of both usability considerations for
newcomers and advanced feature expectations from experienced users.

Onboarding Experience Section
The onboarding and profile setup process received highly positive feedback, with 85.7% of
respondents rating the experience as “5 – Very Easy” and the remaining 14.3% rating it as
“4 – Easy.” No respondents selected ratings between 1 and 3, indicating that the process
was clear and intuitive for all participants. Comments in the follow-up questions confirmed
that users found the layout clean and straightforward, with no reported points of confusion.
Suggested improvements included the option to add multiple dogs during onboarding and,
in one case, the inclusion of all relevant profile information upfront. These insights will help
refine the onboarding process to accommodate multi-pet households while maintaining its
simplicity.

Puppy Mode Result Section
Participants overwhelmingly found the educational content easy to access and
understand, with 71.4% rating it as “very easy” (5) and the remainder (28.6%) rating it as
4 All respondents agreed the educational feature was helpful for new puppy owners.
Additional suggestions included adding obedience training tips, dog-safe treat lists,
grooming guidance, and early signs of health issues.

All respondents confirmed that the puppy profile page clearly displayed mode, vaccination
status, and readiness. No major clarity issues were identified, though one participant
suggested adding links to the dog’s social media profile for a more personal connection.

Social Mode Result Section
71.4% of respondents found the available filters effective in narrowing matches, while
28.6% felt a few important filters were missing. Suggested additional filters included dog
weight and size, as these could influence compatibility, especially in cases where smaller
dogs are intimidated by larger ones. All respondents agreed that dog profiles contained
enough information to make informed decisions about connecting.
Figure C.6: Results from the "Social Mode: filters and profile" (Part 1)

All participants reported that sending and responding to match requests was
straightforward. The chat interface was rated as easy to use by 71.4% (score 5) and
28.6% (score 4). All users found it clear when and why meetups were allowed or
restricted.
Figure C.7: Results from the "Social Mode: match request and chat feature” (Part 2)

The “Meetup” feature was universally considered useful and intuitive (100%). The process
of editing pet and owner profiles was rated as very easy by 85.7% and easy by 14.3%,
with no participants reporting confusion or difficulty.
Figure C.8: Results from the "Social Mode: meet up feature and profile edition" (Part 3)

Overall Result Section
57.1% of respondents rated the app’s design and layout as 4 out of 5, while 42.9% gave
the highest rating of 5. The most liked features included Puppy Mode, meet-up
functionality, mode switching, matchmaking, and profile feedback. Respondents
appreciated the awareness around safe puppy meet-ups, the ability to view detailed dog
profiles, and the sound design.
Figure C.9: Results from the "Overall Impression: design and favourite features" section of
the user survey

Suggestions for improvement included adding support for multiple pets, location mapping
integration, profile rating systems, calendar and meeting reminders, and additional filters
such as age and size. The majority (85.7%) stated they would use the app once launched,
while 14.3% responded “Maybe,” and none indicated they would not use it.
